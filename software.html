Food Systems and Systemic Risk

Agricultural shocks, trade networks, risk propagation, and food security.

AgRichter Scale

Hazard magnitude · Agricultural production

A quantitative framework for characterizing the magnitude of agricultural production disruptions, using spatial production data and harvest–production envelopes to analyze potential losses.

Explore repository →

Virtual Water Trade

Water resources · Global food trade

An R-based framework for tracing virtual water embedded in international agricultural trade, examining blue- and green-water dependencies, trade-network structure, and exposure to water scarcity. The work builds on research themes explored in the 2017 Nature paper “Groundwater depletion embedded in international food trade” (Dalin, Wada, Kastner & Puma).

Explore repository →

Food Shock Cascade

Food systems · Network modeling

The FSC model simulates how production shocks and export restrictions propagate through international agricultural trade networks, examining the roles of trade allocation and food reserves.

Explore repository →

Agrimate–SHEAF

Agricultural markets · Collaboration

Developed in collaboration with Christian Otto and Kilian Kuhla at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK), this extension of Agrimate explores crop substitution, strategic policy responses, and vulnerability to food-market disruptions.

TherapeuticProvisioning

Humanitarian nutrition · Stochastic decision-making

A research model examining how to allocate constrained humanitarian nutrition budgets between ready-to-use therapeutic food (RUTF) and fortified blended food (CSB+). It evaluates procurement choices under uncertain caseloads, early-warning information, transport disruptions, and competing resource needs across illustrative crisis settings.

In development · Unpublished
Exploratory research code and scenarios. Not validated operational guidance for humanitarian procurement or clinical decisions.

Environmental and Agricultural Processes

Modeling soil water, hydrology, landscape dynamics, and crop production.

SoilWaterFlow

Soil hydrology · Plant water uptake

A one-dimensional model of unsaturated soil-water flow and root water uptake, tracing a research lineage from the PLANT1D Fortran model developed with Michael Celia and Andrew Guswa and used in Puma et al. (2007). The modern Python implementation reproduces the original solver and extends it to model competing crop varieties with distinct root systems.

Explore repository →

Spatial Soil

Spatial hydrology · Ecological modeling

A spatial soil-moisture and evapotranspiration model developed from my Princeton doctoral research. It examines how heterogeneous rainfall and vegetation influence root-zone water balances, evapotranspiration, and leakage, with a modern Python implementation and original Fortran code preserved.

Explore repository →

RiverDeltaModel

Geomorphology · Environmental modeling

A cellular model of river-delta evolution, channel avulsions, sediment routing, and three-dimensional stratigraphy. Originally developed in Fortran and subsequently modernized and validated in Python.

Explore repository →

TESSERA

Crop diversity · Resilience

An exploratory model linking crop variety mixtures and field-level environmental variability to regional production stability and local grain-market outcomes. Parameters remain illustrative and uncalibrated.

Explore repository →

Additional research code

Sound Toll Network Analysis examines historical maritime trade through the Danish Sound using the Sound Toll Registers Online. The software analyzes shipping-network connectivity, centrality, and changing trade routes, including comparisons before and after the 1709 Baltic plague.
