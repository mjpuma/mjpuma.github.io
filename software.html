Research Software

Computational models and research tools developed through my work and collaborations, spanning systemic food risks, human mobility, and environmental processes. These projects range from published research code to active model development and exploratory prototypes.

A selected portfolio rather than a complete GitHub listing. Project descriptions identify their scope; links lead to public repositories when appropriate. Collaborative work is credited separately from independently hosted code.

Selected projects

Food Shock Cascade

Food systems · Network modeling

The FSC model simulates how production shocks and export restrictions propagate through international agricultural trade networks, examining the roles of trade allocation and food reserves.

SHEAFFood markets

Agrimate–SHEAF

Agricultural markets · Collaboration

Developed in collaboration with Christian Otto and Kilian Kuhla at the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK), this extension of Agrimate explores crop substitution, strategic policy responses, and vulnerability to food-market disruptions.

Migration & Refugee Network Roles

Mobility · Network analysis

Research code for analyzing international migrant and refugee flow networks, classifying countries' structural roles, and examining relationships with conflict, socioeconomic conditions, drought, and disasters.

AgRichter Scale

Hazard magnitude · Agricultural production

A quantitative framework for characterizing the magnitude of agricultural production disruptions, using spatial production data and harvest–production envelopes to analyze potential losses.

Explore repository →
H₂OTrade flows

Virtual Water Trade

Water resources · Global food trade

An R-based framework for tracing virtual water embedded in international agricultural trade, examining blue- and green-water dependencies, trade-network structure, and exposure to water scarcity. The work builds on research themes explored in the 2017 Nature paper “Groundwater depletion embedded in international food trade” (Dalin, Wada, Kastner & Puma).

Explore repository →

TESSERA

Crop diversity · Resilience

An exploratory model linking crop variety mixtures and field-level environmental variability to regional production stability and local grain-market outcomes. Parameters remain illustrative and uncalibrated.

Explore repository →

RiverDeltaModel

Geomorphology · Environmental modeling

A cellular model of river-delta evolution, channel avulsions, sediment routing, and three-dimensional stratigraphy. Originally developed in Fortran and subsequently modernized and validated in Python.

Explore repository →

Additional research code

Sound Toll Network Analysis examines historical maritime trade through the Danish Sound using the Sound Toll Registers Online. The software analyzes shipping-network connectivity, centrality, and changing trade routes, including comparisons before and after the 1709 Baltic plague. Further research code is available on my GitHub profile.
